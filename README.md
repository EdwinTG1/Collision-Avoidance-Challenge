# Collision-Avoidance-Challenge

Introduction
It is reported that approximately 900,00+ small debris objects with a radius of at least 1cm are currently orbiting uncrollobaly in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). With space travel currently seeing rapid growth, we can expect to see that figure rise; posing a threat to operational satelittes. The European Space Agency released a unique real-world dateset containing time series of events repressenting the evolution of collison risk, related to a number of monitered satelites. This repositry contains a machnine learning pipeline too predict the final risk of collision at time of closet approach between a satellite and a space object, using data cropped at two days to the time of closets approach

The dataset can be downloaded [here] (https://kelvins.esa.int/collision-avoidance-challenge/data/).
